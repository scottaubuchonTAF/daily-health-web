Your log stays yours.

Private storage

Daily Health is a personal habit tracker. Entries are saved in the owner's private Google Sheet through Google Apps Script. The public website contains the interface, icons, and app code. It does not contain log entries.

Google connection

Connecting Google requests permission to use spreadsheets and identify your Google account. The backend limits access to the log's owner. Google access tokens are kept in memory for the current session. This app does not store them in browser storage, cookies, or its public repository.

Signing out of this app clears its connection and loaded entries from the page. It does not sign you out of Google. You can also remove the app's access in your Google account settings.

No advertising or analytics

Daily Health includes no advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub serves the website and Google handles authorization and data storage. Their services may process ordinary connection information under their own policies.

Apple Health and Shortcuts

Apple Health access happens in Shortcuts on your iPhone after you approve it. With automatic imports, your iPhone sends sleep or Watch-step samples over HTTPS directly to the owner's Google Apps Script. The script calculates daily totals and stores them in your private Sheet. It does not retain or log raw samples. A private connection key authorizes only these imports; it cannot read your log or change other habit fields. Only a hash of the key is stored on the server. Keep the key in your private Shortcut and do not share that Shortcut.

Sleep and step On/Off choices are enforced on the server when connected. You can revoke the key in Settings → Apple Health. Recent import totals and manual-edit protections are retained privately in Script Properties for up to 14 days. A status timestamp and counts help you check whether imports arrived. The iPhone automation must be installed once; it runs when its chosen trigger fires, subject to Apple Health access and network availability.

Optional pasted imports are processed in your browser; only selected daily totals are sent to the Sheet. Raw pasted text is cleared when the import closes. Weight exports pass the selected saved weight and date directly to your iPhone Shortcut, which confirms any Apple Health write. Local preferences contain no health values or credentials.

Manage your data

Use History to edit earlier entries and Settings to export a backup. Deleting a day removes it from the app's Sheet; Google's file history or any backups you keep may still contain earlier copies. Your exported backups should be kept private.
